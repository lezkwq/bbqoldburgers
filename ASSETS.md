# Tratamento das imagens

Fotos de origem: materiais fornecidos pelo cliente no chat em 26/10/2026.

As fotos de cardápio foram tratadas com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen. A foto `defumado.webp` preserva a fotografia original, apenas comprimida.

## Prompt utilizado

Edit target: the real [PRODUCT] burger photograph in this BBQ Old Burger’s screenshot. Produce a clean square restaurant menu photo of this same burger. Remove all promotional lettering, graphic decorations, Instagram controls and device interface by cropping around the actual food and gently reconstructing background/plate where necessary. Preserve the actual burger’s shape, ingredients, amounts, colors, textures, relative proportions and position, including any potatoes already present. Do not redesign the food. Natural faithful photographic detail, subtle exposure and clarity improvement, dark restaurant setting, food fully in frame. No added ingredients, no text, no watermarks, no typography, no UI.

Para New York foi usada a mesma orientação, com indicação explícita de preservar mussarela branca, brotos, farofa e pão.
